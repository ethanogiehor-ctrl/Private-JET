# Risks & Constraints

## Context
Our student support platform helps university students find
study partners and organize academic goals. This section
describes possible problems, project limits and how our
team proposes to manage them.

## Content

### Risk 1: Exposure of Private Information
- Problem: Students' private information or goals could
  become visible to other users.
- Response: Limit public profile information and enforce
  access checks so students can only access their own goals.

### Risk 2: Inaccurate or Outdated Profiles
- Problem: Incorrect course codes or old availability could
  lead to unsuitable study-partner matches.
- Response: Validate course codes and allow students to
  update their profiles and availability.

### Risk 3: No Matching Study Partners
- Problem: A student may search for a course with no matches.
- Response: Display a clear message and allow another search.

### Risk 4: Lost or Incorrect Goal Data
- Problem: Goals might not save correctly or may display
  the wrong completion status.
- Response: Test goal creation and updates, and show a clear
  error message when saving fails.

### Constraint 1: Limited Development Time
- The team must complete the project within course deadlines.
- Prioritize study-partner search and basic goal management.

### Constraint 2: Team Experience
- Team members may need time to learn unfamiliar tools.
- Choose manageable technologies and share setup instructions.

### Constraint 3: Available Resources
- Hosting and storage choices depend on available resources.
- Confirm tool costs and usage limits before selecting them.

## Decisions (if any)

- The team still needs to confirm priorities and assign
