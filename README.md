# Team Project
A student support platform that assists uni students in finding study partners and organizing their academic goals.
Project Goals:
Pair Pressure is an app that basically helps university students find study partners with similar courses, schedules, study habits and vibes. It is for students who want company while studying but do not know who to ask. Being in a class with hundreds of people somehow does not make finding study partners easy. Students will create a profile, add their study info and preferences, and swipe through suggested partners. A match happens when both students choose each other, which takes some pressure off the usual awkwardness of introductions. By the end of the course, our product will support profile creation, browsing, and mutual matching. Messaging, AI chat moderation,  session planning, and group matching will follow once the basics work.
